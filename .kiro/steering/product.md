# Product Overview

CareSpot is a world-class NGO website showcasing a community-driven health initiative focused on improving healthcare access, promoting health literacy, and supporting nutrition for underserved populations. The platform serves as the digital face of an organization inspired by SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being) and SDG 2 (Zero Hunger).

## Mission Statement
To bridge gaps in healthcare access, education, and support for underserved communities by promoting preventive care, nutrition, and patient empowerment — with a special focus on vulnerable populations in Ghana and beyond.

## Key Features
- **Impactful Homepage** with hero sections, statistics, and compelling visuals
- **Research Showcase** highlighting RxCare neonatal seizure wearable device research
- **Project Portfolio** featuring health screenings, nutrition programs, and community outreach
- **Donation Platform** with integrated fundraising capabilities
- **Visual Storytelling** with high-quality images and testimonials
- **Mobile-First Design** ensuring accessibility across all devices

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Donors, volunteers, and healthcare advocates
- **Secondary**: Beneficiaries seeking healthcare services
- **Tertiary**: Partners, researchers, and policy makers

## Design Philosophy
Inspired by leading NGO websites like the Red Cross, emphasizing:
- Clean, professional aesthetics
- Emotional storytelling through visuals
- Clear calls-to-action for donations and volunteering
- Accessibility and inclusivity
- Trust-building through transparency
